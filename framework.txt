An slp is a procedure that takes a set of generators of size n 
and outputs another of size m.

A side pairing associates to any edge e of a map G-> S to a surface an automorphism
g of the induced covering X-p^{-1}(V(G)) -> S-V(G) from the universal covering X->S. The notion makes sense when G is the projection
of the boundary of a fundamental domain for S in X and 
when edges of G are loops in S.

The image of a side pairing generates the automorphism group of 
the covering X-p^{-1}(V(G)) -> S-V(G).

Given a map G->S and the dual map G^*-> S-V(G) and a 
spanning tree T in G^*,
denote by G/T -> S the only map with underlying graph in
the dual isomorphic to G^*/T and
such that G/T-> S factors through G -> S. The graph G/T is the gluing of the faces
of G-> S along T.


Normalizations :
	At the beginning we start with a side pairing elts from afuchelts and an
involution from afuchspair associated to elts. Then we build a map G->S
with a new involution without fixed points s1 and a function h with 
side pairing i -> elts(h(i)). The afuch_presentation function
returns an slp, pointers and relations rels that, given elts and
h, can compute a set of generators verifying the relations in 
rels.
