An slp is a function from a set of generators of size n to another of size m.

A side pairing associates to any edge e of a map G-> S to a surface an automorphism
g of the induced covering X-p^{-1}(G) -> S-G from the universal covering X->S.

The image of a side pairing generates the automorphism group.

Given a map G->S and the dual map G^*-> S-V(G) and a covering tree T in G^*,
denote by G/T -> S the only map with underlying graph isomorphic to G^*/T and
such that G/T-> S factors through G -> S. The graph G/T is the gluing of the faces
of G-> S along T.
